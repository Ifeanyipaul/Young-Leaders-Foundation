WHO WE ARE

We believe every child deserves a chance.

Young Leaders Foundation is committed to supporting children and vulnerable communities by creating opportunities that promote care, learning, dignity, and hope.

Our work focuses on reaching children who may face difficult circumstances, including homeless children and orphans. Through our programs and community activities, we strive to make a meaningful difference in their lives.

We believe that positive change begins with compassion and action. By bringing people together, we can provide practical support and help create brighter possibilities for the future.

OUR PURPOSE

Driven by purpose, guided by hope.

Our work is built around a simple belief: every child deserves care, opportunity, and the chance to build a brighter future.

Our Mission

To support children and vulnerable communities by providing practical care, educational opportunities, and meaningful support that can contribute to a brighter and more hopeful future.

Our Vision

To see a future where children, regardless of their circumstances, have access to care, learning, opportunities, and the support they need to thrive.

OUR VALUES

The principles behind our work.

Our values guide how we work with children, communities, volunteers, partners, and everyone who supports our mission.

Compassion

We approach every child and community with kindness, empathy, and genuine care.

Integrity

We value honesty, responsibility, transparency, and doing what is right.

Community

We believe meaningful change happens when people come together and support one another.

Hope

We believe every child deserves opportunities that can help them look forward to a brighter future.

WHAT WE DO

Turning care into real action.

Young Leaders Foundation works to support children and vulnerable communities through practical care, educational support, community engagement, and opportunities that encourage a brighter future.

Supporting Vulnerable Children

Providing care and support for children facing difficult circumstances, including homeless children and orphans.

Feed A Child

Our annual November program focuses on providing food and care to children and vulnerable members of the community.

Teach A Child

Our annual March program supports children's learning and encourages opportunities for knowledge, skills, and confidence.

Community Engagement

Bringing people together to support children and contribute to positive change within communities.
